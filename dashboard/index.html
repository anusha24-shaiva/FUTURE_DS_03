Strategic Action Plan & Funnel Optimization Roadmap

Prioritized data-backed initiatives to plug leakage and maximize revenue per lead

Priority 1 • Quick Win

Optimize Mobile Landing Pages & Reduce Bounce

Mobile conversion lags desktop by 35% (7.8% vs 13.9% lead rate) with high bounce. Implement accelerated mobile pages, frictionless 1-click Google/LinkedIn social login, and sticky CTAs.

Expected Uplift: +12% Visitor→Lead Conv. (+$1.98M ARR)
Priority 2 • Mid-Term

Automate Lead Scoring & BDR Response Time

48.8% of leads drop before MQL due to delayed outreach and manual qualification. Deploy automated routing for leads with score ≥75, guaranteeing contact under 5 minutes.

Expected Uplift: +15% Lead→MQL Conv. (+$2.48M ARR)
Priority 3 • Budget Reallocation

Reallocate Spend from Paid Social to Referral & Email

Paid Social has an unsustainable $15,948 CAC and 2.37x ROAS, whereas Referral and Email boast >44x ROAS and sub-$1,000 CAC. Shift $10,000/mo into co-marketing and partner directories.

Expected Uplift: -38% Blended CAC, +$1.85M ARR
Priority 4 • Sales Enablement

Standardize Security Review & ROI Business Cases

60.8% of Opportunities fail to close, with pricing pushback and compliance approvals cited as top reasons. Provide reps with pre-packaged SOC2/GDPR compliance kits and CFO-ready ROI calculators.

Expected Uplift: +8% Opp→Customer Win Rate (+$1.32M ARR)
