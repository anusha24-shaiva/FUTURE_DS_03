1. Executive Summary

Conversion rate optimization across the marketing and sales funnel is the single highest-leverage growth driver for modern enterprises. This study analyzes an enterprise B2B SaaS marketing funnel tracking 50,000 unique prospective buyer journeys over a 12-month period across 6 acquisition channels.

Key Headline Metrics:
  • Total Pipeline ARR Generated: $16,576,683 across 518 Won Customers ($32,001 average deal ARR).
  • Overall Funnel Conversion Rate: 1.04% (Visitor to Customer), with a 11.83% Traffic-to-Lead conversion rate.
  • Critical Bottleneck: 88.17% of visitors bounce at Stage 1, while 60.85% of active opportunities drop during final contract review.
  • Highest ROI Channels: Email Marketing (95.8x ROAS, $295 CAC) and Referral/Partner (44.4x ROAS, $982 CAC).
